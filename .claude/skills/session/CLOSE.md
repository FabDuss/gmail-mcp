# The closing review and the close

The closing review rides on the last barrier, the barrier of a plan whose `plan.md` leaves no TASK, beside the observation. The close takes the epic from `closing` to `delivered`.

- At the last barrier, launch the architecture critique, subagent type `architecture-critique`, on the epic's diff, unless every work-package of the epic is trivial, or an entry of yours already carries the critique's proposals and no code has landed since. Launch it in the background, like a run, with a prompt that names the epic. A critique that ends without returning its critique, out of turns or crashed, is a lost run: tell the pilot, and relaunch it only when they say so. It reads in your clone and writes nothing: while it runs, your writes touch only `.cairn/`, which it leaves out, and when it returns, `git status` shows no change of its, and you restore any it made.
- When it returns, land its proposals, without its Rejected section, which is its own filtering, in an entry of yours that keeps the status, so that a restart finds them. A critique lost and not relaunched leaves the other items alone. Add to them the rearrangements the architect named in its `designed` entries and the neighbouring bugs that coder notes left. Deduplicate them among themselves, against the open drafts, and against the proposals the pilot rejected or left undecided earlier in this epic, and join into one item the proposals that share a module. Put them to the pilot after the NEEDs, in the format of to-questionnaire: each draft this epic created or added to, with your recommendation to take it up, keep it or drop it, and each proposal with yours to add it to the open draft that carries it, make it a new draft when none does, fix it in this epic, or reject it, as the draft epic section of ideate's [INTENT-FORMAT.md](../ideate/INTENT-FORMAT.md) says. Offer to fix it in this epic only when its Modules line names at least one file and every file it names there is in the epic's diff from main, as `git diff --name-only origin/main...HEAD` lists it. Its evidence may cite any file. A proposal becomes a draft, or an addition to one, only if they approve it, and you write it. When they fix a proposal in this epic, land their other closing decisions first, then send it to the architect by `confirmed`, as a change asked on the PR, open or not.
- The PR goes ready on the acceptance of the NEEDs alone, since a draft touches no code of the epic, unless the pilot fixes a proposal in this epic. The barrier entry records each item the pilot leaves undecided: a draft stays at `draft`, and a proposal left undecided is dropped, as the message tells them.
- A delivered epic resumes here when the pilot asks for main. Check first that its PR is still open: a merged PR means the epic is closed, and a new change takes a new epic; a PR closed unmerged goes to the pilot, who reopens it or abandons the epic. Then land `closing`, set the PR back to a draft PR, or ask the pilot to when no tool can, and close as below.
- At `closing`, main enters again only if it has moved, and the full suite runs again only then or when code landed since it ran. A red result follows the rule of a failing merge, with the fix work-package in the last plan. When main entered, observe again by [OBSERVATION.md](OBSERVATION.md), and put the PR to ready only once the pilot accepts that observation. Their acceptance lands your observation entry, and a rejection follows the path of a rejected observation in your skill.
- Put the PR to ready and land `delivered`. The pilot merges. Worktrees and prototypes are local, so nothing is left to tidy.
- At the pilot's request, at the close of an epic or outside any epic, launch the architecture critique on the whole repository, from a clone at the epic head or, outside any epic, at `origin/main`, with a prompt that says so and gives the pilot's direction, if any. Put each proposal to the pilot as an addition to the open draft that carries it, or as a new draft when none does, as for the closing review, with what the critique adds. What they approve lands as such, and no empty epic is opened.
